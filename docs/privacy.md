# Privacy, concretely

| What | Where it is | Who can read it |
|---|---|---|
| Passwords | The macOS login keychain, as ordinary keychain items tagged `yab` | Yab, under its own signature. Any other app triggers the system's permission dialog. |
| History, bookmarks, open tabs, hidden elements | Small JSON files in `~/Library/Application Support/Yab/` | You. |
| What pages said | The words of each page you visit, in `diary.sqlite` in the same folder, so History finds a page by what it said. Never from a private tab or a page asking for a password | You. Off in Settings › Privacy › History (Remember what pages say). |
| Chats with agents | Each chat and its steps, as files in `chats/` in the same folder | You. With Sync on, your devices too: chats of the last 14 days and pinned ones, the last 80 steps of each, sealed like everything Sync carries; never a chat that mail went into. |
| What agents remember about you | Markdown files in `memory/` in the same folder, to read, correct or delete in Settings › AI › Memory or in any text editor | You. With Sync on, your devices too, but for what a task kept from mail, which stays on its Mac. Off in Settings › AI › Memory. |
| Snippets, and what you paste | Snippets in `snippets.json` beside Mail's stores. For the offer to keep text you paste often, a SHA-256 digest of each paste into a page's box and its dates, a week, in `snippet-pastes.json`; never the words, nothing from a private tab or a password manager's copy | You. Off in Settings › Snippets. |
| Other browsers' history | Read in place, from a copy of each Chromium browser's `History` file, only for a browser you brought in from (or switched on under History from in Settings › Bring In): the field's suggestions, habits, Now and an agent's `history` use it. Never sent anywhere. [docs/other-history.md](other-history.md) | You. Off per browser in Settings › Bring In. |
| Cookies and site data | WebKit's own store for the app | The sites that set them, as in any browser. |
| Extensions | Unpacked in `~/Library/Application Support/Yab/Extensions/`, their data in WebKit's extension store | Each extension, within the permissions you accepted when adding it. |
| Crash reports | macOS's own, in `~/Library/Logs/DiagnosticReports`; Yab's go to its site on the next launch only if you said yes. On an iPhone, the report iOS hands Yab at its next launch (MetricKit), kept in Yab's own folder until you answer | You, and Yab's site once sent. Off until you say yes; Settings › Privacy on the Mac, Settings › About on the iPhone. |
| Daily activity | A random installation ID in Yab's settings, and a random person ID that Sync carries to your other devices end-to-end encrypted; the site stores the IDs, which installations share a person, and the UTC days on which Yab was active, whether it was a Mac or an iPhone, and whether the Mac takes Nightly or Stable updates | You and Yab's site. Off in Settings › Privacy on the Mac (Count me in anonymous usage stats), Settings › About on the iPhone. No pages or searches are sent. |
| Kept Store boosts | Which ones and when each was last counted, in Yab's settings; the site stores how many installations reported each boost on each day, without IDs | You and Yab's site. Off with daily activity. |
| Invites and the member stamp | A random ID for invites alone in Yab's settings. For the stamp (You're No. …), Yab sends that ID, your first name and the first day you had Yab to the site at each launch, whether or not you sent or opened an invite. For invites, the site stores the links you made, the first names sent with them (yours, and a friend's when their Yab opens with your link), the half colors, and for best friends whether each one's Yab was open in the last few minutes, only while you let them see it. [docs/invites.md](invites.md) | You, your friends' pages, and Yab's site. |
| Pages an agent reads | Sent to the agent's model provider while it works on your task | The provider, under its own terms. An agent never sees a private tab. |
| Sync, if you turn it on | Spaces, tabs, bookmarks, settings and a few small files of yours, each sealed on your Mac with a key only your devices have (iCloud Keychain carries it to your other Macs), kept on Yab's sync server as boxes it can't open under names it can't read | Your devices. The server sees how many records there are, their sizes and when they change. [docs/sync.md](sync.md). |
| Handoff | The address and title of the page in front, offered to your iPhone and other Macs signed in to the same Apple ID; never from a private tab, and Yab's servers are not involved. [docs/handoff.md](handoff.md). | Your devices, over Apple's Handoff. |
| Anything else | Nowhere. There is no account. | – |

A **private tab** (`⇧⌘N`) has its own cookie jar, leaves nothing behind when it closes, and is invisible to agents: they can't list, read or act in it.

## What Yab sends

Each request goes to yetanotherbrowser.com and holds what is listed here and nothing more. The site sees the network address of each request, as any web host does; its records keep none.

| Request | When | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| Update check, `GET /appcast.json` | About once an hour while Yab is open, sooner when a release is waiting for this Mac's turn or a critical one couldn't be put in place, on Check Now, when Nightly is turned off, and from Yab's updater when it runs while Yab is closed | The build number, the macOS version and which build of Yab is running, Apple silicon or Intel (`b`, `os`, and `arch`: `arm64` or `x86_64`). `channel=nightly` when this Mac takes Nightly updates. `again=1` once the site has answered a check that UTC day, and on the check made when Nightly is turned off, so the site counts a copy once a day. Once after an update couldn't be put in place, why, in one word (`fail`, such as `download` or `readOnly`). A release spread over hours reaches each Mac at a moment set by a number the Mac rolled once; the number never leaves it. |
| Daily count, `POST /api/activity` | Once on each UTC day Yab is used, while Count me in anonymous usage stats is on | `{"id": …, "person": …, "channel": …}`: the random ID for this installation and the random person ID, made once on a Mac and the same on every device that syncs with it, so the site counts people and not copies. `channel` is the Mac's update channel, `stable` or `nightly`. Yab for iPhone sends its own ID, `"platform": "iphone"` so iPhones are counted apart, and the person ID once its Mac synced it. |
| Kept boosts, `POST /api/kept` | Ten minutes or more after the daily count, each boost at most once in 30 days, while Count me in anonymous usage stats is on | `{"kept": […]}`: the Store ids of boosts kept for a week and still on. No ID. |
| Crash report, `POST /api/crash` | At the next launch after an unexpected quit, if you say yes or Automatically send crash reports is on | macOS's report cut to what finds the crash: the kind of crash, the threads' frames, the code loaded, the versions of Yab and macOS. Why it crashed: the message a failed check or an uncaught exception left, with page addresses, email addresses, names in paths and long runs of hex taken out. The main thread's recent stalls, and where it was in recent hangs. Your home folder is written `~`. From an iPhone, `"platform": "iphone"` and iOS's report (MetricKit) in the same shape: the kind of crash and how it ended, the threads' frames (each binary's name and UUID, the offset in its code), the versions of Yab and iOS, the iPhone model, an uncaught exception's name and the format of its message without what was put into it, and where Yab was in recent hangs. Not the memory around the crash, the region format or the process. |
| Hang counts, `POST /api/hangs` | About once a day, only while Automatically send crash reports is on | How many times Yab stopped responding in the last day, by length, from macOS's own count (MetricKit), with the version, build and macOS. No ID; never the same day twice. |
| Feedback, `POST /api/feedback` | When you send feedback | What you write and attach, the email address if you give one, the versions of Yab and macOS, and the diagnostics if you tick them. |
| Member stamp, `POST /api/invites/stamp` | At each launch, again when a friend's link is claimed, and once a minute while the invites ID can't be read yet. There is no setting for it. | `{"install": …, "name": …}`: the random ID for invites, and your first name: the first word of the name in Autofill › You, else of the Mac account's full name. `since`, when known: the earliest day this Mac had Yab (the day it kept, the first page in History, or the day of its stamp). `same`, only when Sync brought a stamp from another Mac: that Mac's invites ID. |
| Invites, `POST /api/invites/status` | At each launch, and again after an invite is claimed or a best friend is reset | `{"install": …}`: the random ID for invites. |
| Store, `GET /store/…` and `/api/store/requests` | For its list, at most every six hours; when you ask for or vote on a boost, and once a day after that | Nothing for the list and pictures. Asking, voting and the daily look at what became of your asks carry a random voter ID of their own, apart from the installation's, and what you asked for. |
